Knowledge service

A small program you run on this machine (see service/README.md). It owns your profile, your work history and every answer learned from a submitted form, stored as plain markdown you can read, edit or delete. When it is running, Ctrl+Shift+F fills the form in front of you and asks — in the page — about anything it could not answer. When it is not, the extension falls back to its own storage. 



    
Resume held by the service

    

      Two files, two jobs. The document (PDF or DOCX) is what
      gets attached to a form's upload field. The text version
      is what grounds answers the model has to write, since it can read words
      but not a PDF. Send either or both — they are stored separately.
    

    


    
    

    
    

    

      
      
    

    


    


    
OpenRouter (extension-side fallback)

    

      Only used when the knowledge service is off. The service
      has its own key (OPENROUTER_API_KEY in its environment) and
      does the routing and drafting itself — that is the path
      Ctrl+Shift+F uses. This key is for the older in-browser path:
      the popup's Fill (review first) button and Extract with
      AI below. You do not need both. Only the label text
      is ever sent on this path; your answers never leave this browser.
    


    
    

    
    
    

      Avoid router ids like openrouter/free — they pick a different
      model each call, so results and speed vary wildly. Pin one model instead.
    


    
    

    
    
    
    

    


    


    
Resume

    

      Plain-text resume (.txt). Used two ways: parsed
      in the browser to prefill empty profile fields, and — if
      an API key is set above — sent to OpenRouter together with your saved
      answers so the model can draft replies to questions you haven't answered
      before. Without a key it never leaves this machine.
    

    
    

      
      
      
    

    

      Prefill parses the resume in the browser — fast, offline,
      and only as good as the layout. Extract with AI sends the
      whole resume to OpenRouter and reads back work history, education, skills
      and other reusable facts; you review everything before it is saved. Both
      only fill fields that are still empty and never overwrite your edits.

Capture on submit

    

      When you submit a form, its answers are read into a review queue in the
      popup. Nothing is saved until you approve it there, and
      the submit itself is never delayed or altered. Switch this off to go back
      to pressing Save answers yourself.
    

    

    


    
Debug log

    

      Records what the scanner saw — field labels, how each label was found, and
      where it was classified — so a mis-detection can be reported. It stores
      label text and value lengths only, never your answers.
      Off by default; keeps the most recent 400 entries.
